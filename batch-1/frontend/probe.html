Exposures = how many SPHEREx images to load, picked evenly across your dates. 24 is the default and fastest. More images show finer changes over time but take longer, because each one is downloaded from NASA’s archive. Pick All for short date windows, such as an asteroid over 2–3 days. Changing it never changes the science, only how many frames you see.

Moving object? Add its motion (optional)

Proper motion is an object's steady drift across the sky, in milliarcseconds per year (1 mas = 1/3 600 000 of a degree). Leave 0 for things that don't move. The year is when your RA/Dec were true (catalogues usually say 2000).

Type a position, pick a target, or click the sky map.
